## Privacy and directory scope

FurFinder is designed around public-facing entities and organizer-published information. It is not a people-tracking, attendee-tracking or private membership lookup service.

Do not publish private contact details, home addresses, precise personal locations, private group rosters, attendee lists, private conversations or sensitive personal information.

Individual profiles are separate from entity listings. Any such feature must be voluntary, private by default and subject to explicit publication consent and a trusted eligibility process. Public individual profiles are restricted to eligible adults; no public profile creation or publication is provided for minors. A scraped social-media page is not consent to create a FurFinder profile.
