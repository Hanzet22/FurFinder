## Corresponding source for a deployed version

A deployment should be associated with its exact source revision (commit SHA) and build metadata. Do not assume that the newest development branch is the source revision currently running in production or preview.

For a specific deployment, inspect its Vercel deployment metadata and identify the exact commit. The corresponding source can then be viewed or downloaded from GitHub at that commit.
