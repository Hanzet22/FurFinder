## Review method and safeguards

1. Identify the organizer's canonical public website.
2. Compare event date, locality and venue separately; do not infer a field from another source.
3. Record retrieval date and exact source URL for each field.
4. Review terms, robots directives, access controls and rate limits before automated retrieval.
5. Send candidates to human moderation; scraping never auto-publishes.
6. Store public factual metadata and source references only. Do not collect attendee lists, private member rosters, private contacts, private conversations or precise personal locations.
7. Do not republish third-party artwork, logos, photography or promotional copy without an applicable license or permission.
