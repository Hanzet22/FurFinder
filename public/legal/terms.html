2. Eligibility and safety

You may use FurFinder only in a way permitted by the laws and rules that apply to you. Follow each organizer's current age requirements, parental-consent rules, code of conduct and venue policies. A listing does not mean every part of an external event or website is suitable for every age group. Do not attempt to bypass age gates or identity checks.

3. Acceptable use

  • Use the directory respectfully and lawfully.
  • Do not submit private addresses, private meetup locations, personal phone numbers, private email addresses or information about another person without permission.

  • Do not use FurFinder to arrange unsafe or unlawful activity.

Suggestions are not automatically published. FurFinder may verify, edit for clarity, reject, archive or remove a listing. Submit only information intended for public discovery and provide official source links. Do not include confidential or sensitive personal information.
